⌨️ Help & Tips

Zoom: Pinch gesture · scroll wheel · +/− keys · slider in controls

Pan: 1-finger drag on map · Arrow keys · D-pad in controls · enable Drag-to-pan

Mobile: Swipe ↑ bottom bar · tap controls icon in nav · use 2-finger drag to pan view

Desktop: Scroll = zoom · Panel toggle with S key

Theme: T key or moon icon

💡 The embedded map is fully interactive — click markers, Street View, zoom controls inside the map all work!
