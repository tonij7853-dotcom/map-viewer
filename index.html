⌨️ Help & Tips

Zoom: Pinch gesture · scroll wheel · +/− keys · zoom slider in Controls

Pan: 1-finger drag on map · Arrow keys · D-pad in Controls · enable Drag-to-pan

Double-tap: Zoom in 2× — double-tap again to reset

Mobile: Tap 🎛️ Controls in nav bar · pinch to zoom · 2-finger drag to pan

Desktop: Scroll wheel zooms at cursor · S key opens panel

💡 The map inside is fully interactive — click markers, Street View, the built-in zoom all work!
